This service combines two things into one: a RAG pipeline that answers
questions from your own documents, and an agent that can also search the
live web or do exact math when your documents alone aren't enough.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) works by splitting documents into
chunks, converting each chunk into a vector embedding, and storing them in
a searchable index. When a question comes in, it's embedded the same way,
compared against the index, and the most relevant chunks are pasted into
the prompt before the model answers.

An agent adds a decision loop on top of that: instead of only ever
answering from documents, it can choose between multiple tools -- searching
this document index, searching the live web, or running a calculation --
based on what the question actually needs.
